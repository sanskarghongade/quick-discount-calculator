How the discount maths works

A percentage discount is just a fraction of the original price. For example, 20% of 1,000 is 200, so the final price is 800.

  • Single discount: final price = original price − (original price × discount ÷ 100).
  • Successive discounts: every discount applies to the price that is already reduced, so you multiply the amounts that remain: original × (1 − d1/100) × (1 − d2/100) and so on. Two 10% discounts give 0.9 × 0.9 = 0.81, which is a 19% total discount, not 20%.
  • Comparing offers: work out the final price of each offer and pick the lower one.
