Frequently asked questions about discounts

How do I calculate a discount on a price?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to get the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price. For example, 20% of 1,000 is 200, so the final price is 800.

How do I calculate successive discounts?

Apply each discount to the price that is already reduced. For a price of 1,000 with 20% then 10%, the first discount leaves 800, and the second discount leaves 720.

Are two 10% discounts the same as 20% off?

No. Successive discounts are multiplied, so two 10% discounts give 0.9 × 0.9 = 0.81. That is a 19% total discount, slightly less than a single 20% discount.

How do I compare two discount offers?

Work out the final price of each offer and compare the results. The offer with the lower final price is the better deal, even if its advertised discount percentage looks smaller.

Yes, it is completely free and needs no sign-up. Every calculation runs inside your browser, so nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.
